Research model

AI’s most useful job here is not to tell us what the past means.

It is to put evidence humans have traditionally studied in separate worlds onto a shared clock and a shared map — then stop.

Keep the classes apart

A measured isotope spike is not an ancient story. A historical text is not a magnetometer reading. A crop formation is an observed pattern on the ground; its proposed meaning is an interpretation. Sensor footage is not a velocity. A royal inscription is not pollen.

STRATA will still place these streams in the same frame, because the only honest question that can be asked across them is: do independently generated indicators repeatedly converge around the same periods or places?

If they do, we investigate. If they do not, the hypothesis weakens. If the relationship survives controls, dating uncertainty, alternative explanations, and independent reproduction, then we have something more interesting than coincidence. Until then, we do not promote.

The chain is a hypothesis

Solar state → geomagnetic environment → atmospheric or electromagnetic conditions → climate proxies → archaeological change → historical observation. That sequence is a model to test. It is not a conclusion to assume, and it is not a license to read every omen as a particle event.

The contemporary version is the same discipline applied to UAP records: timestamp, place, sensor, morphology, solar indices, moon, aircraft, and control nights. Headline flares are not the test. Continuous state is. Tehran on 20 September 2026 does not sit on a major CME. That is useful. It kills the cartoon version of the idea without killing the broader one.

What the machine is for

  • Find candidate connections humans may not have noticed across disciplinary boundaries.
  • Freeze the data. Hash the snapshot. That is a receipt, not a truth.
  • List alternatives. Widen dating error until the cluster evaporates — or does not.
  • Run a null of shuffled years. Compare event dates to control dates.
  • Leave identification to independent evidence and reproduction.

Humans interrogate. Independent records test. Reproduction decides what survives. Reality retains veto.

Promotion ladder

  • E0Anecdote. No receipt.
  • E1Single-source observation.
  • E2Documented with provenance.
  • E3Independently corroborated within class.
  • E4Multi-class temporal adjacency.
  • E5Multi-class, geographic, survives controls.

Individual records in this atlas are scored as records (E0–E3). Multi-class adjacency is a property of a cluster, not a reason to upgrade a chronicle into a measurement. E5 is empty on purpose until something survives.

What this is not

It is not a disclosure engine, a decoded Bible, a crop-circle grammar, or a proof of solar-triggered craft. It is a laboratory bench: labeled drawers, a shared clock, a null model, and a rule that the most interesting sentence in the room is still allowed to fail.
