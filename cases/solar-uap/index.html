The hypothesis under test — not a finding.

The atlas will overlay solar state on observational events. Overlay is not correlation. Correlation is not mechanism. Mechanism is not identification.

    weakened

    live

    This is the broader claim. It needs event dates vs control dates, not anecdotes.

  • H3 · No relationship above reporting bias and sky-object confusion

    live

    The null. Must remain available. Moon, Venus, aircraft, kites, and drones are controls, not insults.
